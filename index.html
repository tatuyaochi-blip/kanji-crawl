  1. A yokai appears with a kanji. Pick the right answer to attack. Answer fast for a critical hit.
  2. Wrong answers or running out of time let the enemy hit you. The correct answer is shown so you learn it.
  3. After each fight, choose one of three relics. Each floor ends with a boss built from your weakest kanji.
  4. Your progress on every kanji is saved between runs. Floor 2 adds readings, floor 3 asks you to find the kanji from its meaning.

Keys: 1–4 to answer, H for a hint.
