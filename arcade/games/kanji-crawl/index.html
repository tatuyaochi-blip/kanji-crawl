A roguelite where every attack is a Japanese kanji (JLPT N5).

About the game

Kanji Crawl is a short roguelite for Japanese learners. Descend three floors of a yokai-filled dungeon and answer kanji questions to attack: meanings first, then readings, then pick the right kanji from its meaning. Choose relics between fights, and face bosses built from the kanji you keep missing. Progress is saved in your browser, and a daily dungeon is the same for everyone.

How to play

Tap or click an answer. Keyboard: 1–4 answer, H hint.
